# Design System

## Brand
Premium biology EdTech / private tutor.

## Visual direction
- calm editorial layout;
- deep forest green;
- warm paper background;
- muted mint surfaces;
- gold as evidence/accent, not decoration;
- large editorial typography;
- rounded geometry without excessive glassmorphism;
- evidence cards visually stronger than marketing copy.

## UX priorities
1. Photo, positioning and CTA above the fold.
2. Verified results before prices.
3. Clickable evidence.
4. Visible disclaimer: no guaranteed score.
5. Mobile-first navigation.
6. Strong focus states and reduced-motion support.

## Components
- Hero stage
- Evidence score rail
- Result feature card
- Progress roadmap
- Credential gallery
- Pricing cards
- Interactive materials rows
- Modal viewer
- Lead form
